02Approval flowflow - 74 words

The important pattern is to approve scope before implementation. That keeps execution bounded and makes the final report easier to audit.

Scope request

Define the desired outcome, audience, constraints, and non-goals.

Scope approval

Human approves exactly what can be built or reported.

Implementation

Work happens inside the approved boundary with receipts captured.

Implemented report

The final bundle separates claims, evidence, residual risk, and next actions.

03Implemented report anatomysections - 101 words

An implemented report should be skimmable from the nav and still complete when every section is opened.

  • OverviewWhat changed, why it matters, and current status.
  • Scope and approvalsWho approved what, when, and which boundaries remained out of scope.
  • Implementation summaryThe concrete work performed, grouped by component or workflow.
  • Validation evidenceTests, readbacks, screenshots, and source links. Summarize logs; do not dump them.
  • ArtifactsHTML, PDF, bundle files, deploy URLs, and source paths.
  • Risks and next actionsKnown gaps, owner decisions, and follow-up tasks.

01Purposescope - 204 words

Turn the 77 /apps directories in the Disler repositories into a ranked, queryable exemplar library of products, grounded in what Hermes already runs, so that when GBAutomation builds or improves one of its own product apps the best-matching exemplar, its Hermes counterpart, its effort and its read paths come back in one query. Greg's rulings (2026-09-30): the /apps value is a targeted subset of examples to leverage, Hermes is the runtime priority, and an app means a product. This document is a plan only. Nothing here is built, dispatched, applied or armed.

02Problemgap - 220 words

The /apps pattern was never identified as a TAC component. Evidence from the committed discovery (artifacts/tac-apps-standard-discovery/2026-09-30/discovery-report.md, base b0daca858):

  • Wrong unit. The TAC knowledge base and engineering catalog index files. An app is a directory; 408 KB components carry an apps/ prefix and 99 an app/ prefix but there is no app primitive, archetype facet or search document type.
  • Vocabulary collision. mini app is already our route mini-app-to-full-repo and a build-target term; it occurs 0 times in the Disler Markdown and 0 times in tac-kb.md. Re-run today, tac_knowledge_query.py "mini app" returns 13 token hits on the word app and no app.
  • Mapper throws structure away. resources/lib/tac_engineering.py:853 keeps a flat apps/<name> list, misses singular app/ (tac-2 to tac-7) and nested */apps/* (tac-8).
  • Our own products lack the shape. Of our 11 product-shaped apps/ dirs: 0 have a justfile, 1 has specs/, 0 have both specs/ and app_docs/, 2 have an owner expert.
  • The July 2026 plan (per-app adws/, first slice build-analytics) never started and contradicts both Disler's repo-root agentic layer and the Hermes-first runtime ruling.

Greg's first draft (tiered T0/T1/T2 expert apps) was superseded by his two rulings. The exemplar library replaces it: it only describes and grounds, adds no process, port, store, schedule, card or plugin.

Why a sibling projection. The tracked search index is 3.96 MB and 3,406 documents. Appending exemplar documents to it would churn a tracked artifact and risk golden-query drift for every existing query. A sibling file merged at query time leaves the tracked index byte-identical, so the regression check is exact rather than statistical.

Production end state (Greg's build-to-production rule): the library is merged to main, the projection is regenerated and committed, and the Hermes side consumes it through the existing runtime promotion path. Promotion to MM1 and the pilot's live-portal deployment are named end-state steps that each carry their own approval (section 17); no cron is added.

06Retrieval Designdesign - 295 words
  • Units. application_units() returns path, archetype (rule-based over manifests), entrypoints, ports (from env samples), structure flags (README, justfile, env sample, tests, specs, adws, app_docs, .claude, .pi), expert embedding and agentic layer (root or in-app). Output: artifacts/tac-exemplar-library/application-units.json.
  • Projection. tac_engineering.py build-exemplars (new subcommand, deterministic, no network) writes artifacts/tac-engineering-search/tac-engineering-exemplars.json: one exemplar document per record plus one exemplar-index document. Facets: exemplar-archetype/*, product-role/{product,reference}, hermes-counterpart/{adopted,partial,gap}, adaptation-effort/{S,M,L}, exemplar-status/*.
  • Query. tac_knowledge_query.py "<q>" --exemplars merges the sibling projection at query time and renders title, archetype, pattern, hermes_counterpart (status and refs), effort and read paths. Without the flag, output is unchanged.
  • Synonyms. Exact-phrase keys only: mini app, mini-app, apps directory, apps/, product app, example app, demo app, app folder. No single-token key such as app, so existing queries cannot shift. A hit on the route mini-app-to-full-repo is labelled as a promotion route beside the exemplars.

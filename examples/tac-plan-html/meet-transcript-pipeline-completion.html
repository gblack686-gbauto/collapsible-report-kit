01Overview87 words - 89 words

Today meet-transcript-heartbeat genuinely works end to end through evidence extraction, but the entire second half of what a meeting needs is missing: no Supabase persistence, no report written back to Drive, no attendee email (a hard gmail_disabled: true in the worker's own preflight), and no way to know that a first-time visitor from the website ever showed up. This plan closes that gap by wrapping the existing pipeline, not replacing it, and by reusing every piece of internal infrastructure that already exists rather than inventing parallel systems.

02Operator Recommendation82 words - 83 words

Apply the dormant source_artifacts migration first (it has existed since 2026-06-18 and was never turned on, confirmed live on 2026-09-22 via to_regclass), then build outward from that verified foundation: report write-back, the human-gated Drive permission grant, the new client index, per-expert board provisioning, the proposal trigger, and justfile hygiene. Every phase reuses a component that is already live in this repository — gmail_send_policy.py, run_portfolio_proposals.py/run_proposals.py, deploy_expert.py's one-command pattern, and the tac-human-approval-resume-contract gate shape — rather than authoring new equivalents.
